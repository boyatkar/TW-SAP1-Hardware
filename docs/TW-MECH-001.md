# TW-MECH-001 -- Mechanical Design Specification

**Project:** TW-SAP1 Hardware Platform **Product:** XS3400 Residential
Smart Lock **Revision:** A (Draft)

## Purpose

Defines the mechanical envelope and interfaces for the XS3400.

## Mechanical Envelope

-   Main PCB: 65 × 45 mm
-   4×AA battery compartment
-   Tempered glass front panel
-   Hidden capacitive keypad
-   Front-mounted HLK-ZW101 fingerprint sensor

## Mechanical Interfaces

-   MinebeaMitsumi actuator (preferred)
-   Dormount Euro profile cylinder (Phase 1)
-   USB-C emergency power access
-   Mechanical key override

## PCB Constraints

-   Four mounting points
-   RF keep-out at ESP32-C6 antenna
-   Cable routing clearance
-   Serviceable connectors

## Environmental Targets

-   Indoor/outdoor residential use
-   Moisture-resistant front assembly
-   Fire-door variant to be considered in future platform revisions
