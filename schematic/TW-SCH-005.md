# TW-SCH-005 -- DRV8833 Motor Driver

## Objective

Drive the MinebeaMitsumi actuator.

### Interfaces

-   PWM/GPIO from ESP32
-   VMOTOR input
-   Motor connector

### Requirements

-   Flyback handled by IC
-   Wide motor traces
-   Current measurement provision (future)

### Deliverables

-   DRV8833
-   Motor connector
-   Power decoupling
